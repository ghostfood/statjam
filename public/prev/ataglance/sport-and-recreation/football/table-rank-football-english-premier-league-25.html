English Premier League football clubs

48 clubs competed in the Premier League between 1992 and 2017

In order of success: 1992-2017

Explanation

This ranking is based on a weighted value system. Points were awarded to each club based on their final league table position per season e.g. 125 points for finishing first, 75 points for finishing second, 60 points for finishing third and so on with the bottom three clubs receiving nine, six and three points (with three, two and one point(s) for the bottom three in the initial 22 club Premier League seasons).
