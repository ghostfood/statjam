Key for terminology and symbols

Label Explanation
Type C - Chairman; CEO - Chief Executive Officer (Afghanistan only); CLR - Chancellor; CR - Captains Regent (San Marino only); FCL - Federal Council member; FS - First Secretary; GEN - Governor-General; GS - General Secretary; HG - Head (or Chief) of Government; MS - Minister of State (Monaco only); PRES - President; PRES-G - President of Governorate (Vatican City only); PM - Prime Minister or Premier; SOV - Sovereign (hereditary); SOV E - Sovereign (elected); SOV PM - Sovereign also Head of Government; SOV UK - Sovereign is based in the United Kingdom.
Began The year the individual's leadership started.
Terms The total number of periods of an individual's leadership between elections or in the case of sovereigns the entire duration of their leadership.
* indicates clarification or context recommended for this information e.g. an individual's leadership or a particular term of it may have been reported as politically illegitimate or there was a transfer of leadership between elections.

Key for candidate's electoral term status

Work in progress
Candidate appointed amidst allegations of either electoral fraud, corruption and/or negligence of due process.
Candidate appointed by agreement reached amidst allegations of either electoral fraud, corruption and/or negligence of due process.
Candidate appointed unopposed during predecessor's term - yet to contest leadership election or Parliamentary election.
Candidate appointed following leadership election victory.
Candidate appointed following legitimate Parliamentary election victory.
Candidate appointed following Parliamentary election victory accepted by independent observers as legitimate despite irregularities.
