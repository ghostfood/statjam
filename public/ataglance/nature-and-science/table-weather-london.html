Additional information

The primary purpose of this page is to allow for comparisons to be made of the weather on specific days of the year in a specific location. In the table provided this can be done by entering in the search filter field the day and month of a year as they are displayed in the first column of the table. Entering e.g. '0301' will filter results to show the weather for the 1st of March on each year since 2010. Ultimately this model could include data spanning a larger range of both time and locations.

Links in the Photo Link column go to search results on Flickr with the criteria as follows: Orientation: Landscape or panorama; Minimum size: Medium; Date taken: the corresponding date in the same table row (e.g. 2017 03 31); Content: Photos; Tags: london, thames. You can alter these criteria for more or less accurate results regarding evidence of weather conditions on any given day within the range presented on this page.
