    Explanation of selected terminology and symbols

    Label Explanation
    Year of accession
    Year of the monarch's accession to the throne prior to coronation
    Issue (leg.)
    Number of children born to the monarch and recognised as legitimate heirs by virtue of having been conceived in wedlock.
    Issue (illeg.)
    Number of children born to the monarch deemed illegtitimate due to having conceived out of wedlock.
    W
    Monarch was a woman
    Uncrowned
    Monarch recognised as having acceeded to the throne but with no subsequent coronation.
    Disputed
    Subject's claim to the specific is disputed, in most if not all cases indicating they ruled over a majority of the realm, not its entirety.
