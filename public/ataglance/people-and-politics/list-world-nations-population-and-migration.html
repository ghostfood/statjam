Populations and migration

Facts and figures on the density and movement of people around the world

Net migration in high-GDP nations since 1960

Migration, specifically immigration, is a frequent topic of news coverage and editorial opinion across media of many nations but particularly those regarded as wealthy (as measured by their Gross Domestic Product), whereby that wealth is largely dependent on maintaining a proportionate influx or net gain of migrants to ensure industrial growth.

The ratio of people by nation and global region

Including only nations larger than 10,000km2 and/or with a population of over 1,000,000. Population density for entire nations can be misleading as it tends to be higher in larger cities and regions with more industrial activity, therefore reflecting the quality of a nation's distribution of its population rather than directly indicating whether its population is too high or low for the territory it covers or its level of urbanization and industrial development.
