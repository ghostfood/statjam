Argentina
ARG

In 2026 Argentina won every match en route to the final for the first time in their history.

In 2018 Argentina recorded their heaviest defeat in a group stage match since 1958, ending a streak of eleven consecutive World Cup group games unbeaten and extending a run of World Cup matches without a victory in normal time to four.

Argentina also competed in the 1930 World Cup, beating France, Mexico, Chile and the United States before losing to Uruguay in the final. Argentina lost to Sweden in the opening knockout round of the 1934 World Cup and failed to qualify for the 1938 World Cup.
