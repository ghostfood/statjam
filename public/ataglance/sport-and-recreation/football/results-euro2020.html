Italy wore their dark blue second strip shorts despite their white first strip shorts not clashing with Spain's dark blue first strip shorts. Spain wore their entire second strip despite needing only to have worn their white second strip socks with their first strip shirt and shorts to avoid any clash with Italy's first strip.
