UK Chart hits editorial guide

Ampersands
- Ampersands are used when the artist name consistently features two or more acts (whether using their real names or not) of equal billing (e.g. Simon & Garfunkel; Chase & Status) or a lead vocalist with a backing group (e.g. 'Bill Haley & His Comets'; 'Florence & The Machine'). For guest artists, even of equal billing, a comma is instead used to separate credited acts on a track e.g. 'Coldplay, B.T.S.', 'Dr. Dre, Snoop Dogg'.

Apostrophes
- Apostrophes are only used in the possessive sense, not to indicate a plural
