

If the top party's vote share decreased while the runner-up party's share increased

Add the decrease of the top party and the increase of the runner-up party together then divide by 2

---

If the top two parties vote shares both increased

In this instance we take the smallest increase (5.3) from the largest (8.5) which leaves us 3.2 which we again divide by 2 and the resulting 1.6 per cent is the swing from Conservative to Lib Dem in that seat.
